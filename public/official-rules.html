The Promotion is open only to legal residents of the State of Washington who are 18 years of age or older at the time of entry. 3. Promotion Period

The Promotion begins at 12:00 PM Pacific Time on April 24, 2026, when the Washington State Evergreen Spring Home Show opens in Monroe, Washington, and ends at 11:59 PM Pacific Time on May 15, 2026 ("Entry Period"), or upon receipt of 100 valid entries, whichever is later. The primary Sponsor's computer is the official time-keeping device for this Promotion.

4. How to Enter

There are three (3) equally weighted methods of entry, all of which are free. Limit one (1) entry per person, regardless of method used. Duplicate entries from the same person will be void.

4.1 Online Entry

Visit coreyihlerrealestate.com/monroe-giveaway.html, complete the official entry form, agree to the required terms, and submit.

4.2 In-Person Entry

Visit the Sponsors' booth at the Washington State Evergreen Spring Home Show held at the Evergreen State Fairgrounds Event Center, 14405 179th Avenue SE, Monroe, WA 98272, between April 24, 2026 and April 26, 2026, and complete a paper entry card or the tablet-based online form.

4.3 Alternative Method of Entry (AMOE) — Mail

To enter without internet access or attending the event, hand-print your full name, complete mailing address, email address, phone number (optional), date of birth, and the statement "I am a Washington State resident and at least 18 years of age" on a plain 3x5-inch card or piece of paper, and mail it in a #10 envelope with first-class postage to:

Monroe Home Show Giveaway
c/o Corey Ihler

Mail-in entries must be postmarked no later than May 12, 2026, and received no later than May 15, 2026, to be eligible. All methods of entry will be pooled equally for the drawing. Mechanically reproduced entries are not permitted.
